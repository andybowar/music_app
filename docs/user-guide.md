# Worship Library user guide

The Worship Library is where People of Praise branches keep the songs they use, along with
chord charts, recordings and projection files. You can search it, build setlists for events and
listening playlists, and share them with your team.

This guide is for everyone who uses the app. The sections are:

- [Getting started](#1-getting-started): everyone
- [Finding songs](#2-finding-songs): everyone
- [Setlists and playlists](#3-setlists-and-playlists): everyone; music leaders do more with setlists
- [Sharing a list](#4-sharing-a-list): list owners and music leaders
- [Other branches](#5-other-branches-and-quick-theme-lookup) and [quick theme lookup](#5-other-branches-and-quick-theme-lookup): everyone
- [Adding and editing songs](#6-adding-and-editing-songs): contributors, music leaders and admins
- [Reporting a bug](#7-reporting-a-bug): everyone
- [Admin tools](#8-admin-tools): admins, and music leaders for themes

## Who can do what

Your role is set by an admin when your account is approved.

| You can…                                                           | Member | Contributor | Music leader | Admin |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | :----: | :---------: | :----------: | :---: |
| Search the library, open charts, lyrics and recordings             |   ✓    |      ✓      |      ✓       |   ✓   |
| Make your own setlists and playlists, and share them               |   ✓    |      ✓      |      ✓       |   ✓   |
| See every branch's setlists                                        |   ✓    |      ✓      |      ✓       |   ✓   |
| Change and share any setlist from your branch                      |        |             |      ✓       |   ✓   |
| Add songs, edit tags and lyrics, upload files, set branch usage    |        |      ✓      |      ✓       |   ✓   |
| See a song's change history                                        |        |      ✓      |      ✓       |   ✓   |
| Merge themes and set search synonyms                               |        |             |      ✓       |   ✓   |
| Approve accounts, manage users and branches, use the admin backlog |        |             |              |   ✓   |

Playlists are private: only you (and admins) can see yours unless you share a link to one.

## 1. Getting started

### Requesting an account

1. On the sign-in page, choose **Request access**.
2. Enter your name, email and a password (at least 8 characters), then press **Request access**.
3. An admin reviews every request. Until yours is approved, signing in shows only an
   **Account status** page ("Awaiting approval"). You get an email when you're approved.

If an admin invites you, you get an email with a sign-in link and your account is approved
straight away.

### Signing in

- **With your organization account:** choose **Continue with Google**, **Continue with
  Microsoft** or **Sign in with your organization** (enter your work email), whichever your
  branch uses. If you already have an account with the same email, it is linked automatically.
- **With email and password:** enter them and press **Sign in**. If you've forgotten your
  password, choose **Forgot your password?** and follow the link in the email.

After signing in you land on the **Worship Library**.

### Getting around

The bar at the top of every page (and the tab bar at the bottom on a phone) has:

- **Home**: the whole library, newest songs first. It clears any search you had.
- **Search library**: jumps to the search box.
- **Open setlist** / **Open playlist**: your lists and your branch's lists.
- **Create event setlist** / **Create listening playlist**.
- **Share**: shares your _current list_ (see [section 3](#3-setlists-and-playlists)). It is
  greyed out until you have one.
- **More**: **Quick theme lookup**, **Other branches**, and **Add song** if you can add songs.

The account button (top right) shows who you're signed in as and your branch. It also has
**Report a bug**, **Admin** (admins and music leaders) and **Sign out**.

### Using it on your phone

On an iPad or iPhone, a banner at the top of the app offers to show you how to add it to your Home Screen (Share, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**). Once you've added it, open it from the new icon and sign in again: the Home Screen app keeps its own sign-in. Tap ✕ on the banner to hide it for good on that device.

The app works on any phone from 360 pixels wide. To get an icon on your home screen, use your
browser's **Add to Home Screen** or **Install app** option.

Setlists you have opened on a device, and the PDFs you opened from them, stay available
**offline**. If you lose signal at an event, open the setlist from the same device. A banner
tells you that you're offline and when the copy was saved. Pages you never opened show a short
"you're offline" page with links to your saved setlists. Signing out, or opening the sign-in page,
clears the offline copies.

## 2. Finding songs

### Searching

Type in **Search songs** and press Enter. Below the box, choose which fields to search:
**Titles** (alternate titles included), **Lyrics**, **Themes** and **Composer**. At least one
must stay on. To find songs by theme only, for example "redemption", turn the others off.

If you misspell a title, the library still tries to find close matches and may ask **Did you
mean…?**

### Filtering and sorting

Use **Filters** to narrow the list. You can combine as many as you like, and the results update
as you choose:

- **Energy** (one or more of the five levels below)
- **Key** (matches any key a song has, not only its usual key)
- **Decade** written
- **Branch use**: a branch and how often it sings the song
- **First chord: chorus/refrain**, **First chord: verse**, **First chord: bridge**

**Clear filters** removes them all. Sort by **Relevance** (while searching), **Date added**,
**A–Z**, **Primary key**, **Energy** or **Branch use**, and use the arrow button to reverse the
order.

Your search lives in the page address, so **Back** returns you to your previous results, and you
can send a search to someone as a link.

### Energy levels

| Level                 | Color  | Feel                       |
| --------------------- | ------ | -------------------------- |
| High Energy 'Praise'  | red    | Fast, loud, celebratory    |
| High Energy 'Worship' | yellow | Big and full, often slower |
| Mid Energy 'Worship'  | green  | Steady and singable        |
| Deep 'Worship'        | purple | Slow and reverent          |
| Quiet 'Worship'       | blue   | Very soft and intimate     |

The level is also written out, so you never have to rely on color.

### Reading a song row

Each row shows, from left to right: the tempo (BPM) on a chip colored by energy, the time
signature, the usual key, the title, the composer (People of Praise composers are highlighted),
the year, **Resources**, the code of the branch that added it, and **ADD**. Tap a part of the
row to see more:

- **Key**: every key the song has, with the first chords of the intro, verse, chorus and bridge.
- **Title**: the first line of the chorus and of verse 1.
- **Composer**: all the composers.
- **Resources**: lyrics, chord charts, lead sheets, scores, recordings, and projection files or
  links. PDFs open in a viewer in the app, recordings play in place, projection files download,
  and links open in a new tab. Files open only for signed-in users.
- **Branch code**: how often each branch sings the song.
- **ADD**: adds the song to a list (see below).

Each panel also links to the song's own page. That page shows everything about the song: lyrics
in the order they're sung, keys and first chords, themes, files and branch usage. Contributors
and above also see a **History** tab listing who changed what, and when.

## 3. Setlists and playlists

- A **setlist** is for a dated event, such as a Lord's Day or a prayer meeting. You can change a
  song's key, tempo or energy for that event without changing the library.
- A **playlist** is for listening. Each song plays the recording you pick.

### Creating a list

Choose **Create event setlist** or **Create listening playlist**, enter a name (and the event
date for a setlist), and save. The new list becomes your **current list**, and you go to the
library to add songs.

### Adding songs

Press **ADD** on any song row. You can add it to:

- **Current**: your current list, if you have one ("Event: …" for a setlist, "Listen: …" for a
  playlist).
- **Existing**: another list you can change.
- **New**: a new setlist or playlist, created on the spot.

After adding, you stay on your search results.

### Opening a list

**Open setlist** and **Open playlist** show your lists, with upcoming events first. For setlists,
switch between **My branch** and **All branches** to see what other branches are planning. Use
**Set as current** to make a list your current list.

### Working on a setlist

If you own the setlist, or you're a music leader in its branch, you can:

- **Reorder** songs: drag the handle, or use a song's **…** menu and choose **Move up** or
  **Move down**. With a keyboard, focus the handle, press Space, move with the arrow keys, then
  press Space again.
- **Change key, BPM or energy** for this event, from the song's **…** menu. For the key you can
  also tap the song's key, pick one, and choose **Use … for this event** (or **Back to the usual
  key**). The changed values
  are marked. If the library has no chords or files for the new key, the first chords show **X**
  and you get a link to upload files for that key.
- **Remove from setlist**, from the song's **…** menu; **Rename** the setlist or change its date;
  and **Delete** it.

Everyone else can view setlists but not change them.

### Playing a setlist

On a setlist, tap **▶ Play** on any song. The player opens full screen on that song, and
**Previous** / **Next** move through the rest of the set.

Each song shows its key, BPM and the first chords for the setlist's key, then a chart. If the
song has more than one (typed chords, chord charts, lead sheets or scores), you pick one each time
the song comes up; **Choose another chart** goes back to the list. PDFs show full width with every
page one under another, and **Zoom** opens the page-by-page viewer. With no chart, you get the
lyrics and first chords. If typed chords are in a different key from the setlist, a warning says
so. **A−** and **A+** change the text size of lyrics and typed chords.

Turn on **Autoscroll** and set its **Speed** from 1 (slow) to 10. The speed is remembered for each
song on your device, and autoscroll turns off when you move to another song.

**Capo:** if the chords are hard to play (lots of flats, say), use **Capo** − / + to set the fret
your capo is on, from 1 to 7. The song stays in the same key; each chord shows the **shape to
play**, with the chord that sounds small beside it, e.g. **G** (A♭), and the line under the
buttons says which shapes you're playing ("Capo 1 · play G shapes · key A♭"). **Suggest** picks
the fret that gives the fewest sharps and flats. The capo changes typed chords and first chords
(PDF charts stay as written), works on the song page too, and is remembered for each song and key
on your device.

Move between songs with **Previous** and **Next**, by swiping sideways, or with the arrow or Page
Up/Page Down keys, which is what most Bluetooth page-turner pedals send. A pedal press first
scrolls the song down a screen, and moves to the next song once you're at the end.
The screen stays on while the player is open, and setlists you've opened before still work
offline.

### Playing a playlist

On a playlist, pick a **Recording** for each song from its menu. You can choose an uploaded
recording or a YouTube or audio link. Then press **Play all**. The player at the bottom has play,
pause, stop, previous and next, and it moves on to the next song by itself. Songs without a
recording are skipped.

## 4. Sharing a list

You can share any list you're allowed to change. Other people don't need an account to see a
shared list, but they do need to sign in to open its files.

1. Open the list and press **Share**. Or press **Share** in the top bar, which shares your
   current list.
2. Optional: choose an **Expiry** (**Never expires**, **Expires in 7 days** or **Expires in 30
   days**).
3. Press **Create link**, then **Copy link**, and send it however you like.

The full link is shown **only once**. For security the app doesn't keep it, so copy it straight
away. If you lose it, create another link.

The person you send it to sees a read-only copy of the list, including any key, tempo and energy
changes you made for the event.

**To email the list**, type up to 20 addresses under **Email the list**, separated by commas, and
press **Send email**. Each person gets a summary of the list and a link that never expires.
Nobody sees the other recipients' addresses. You can send up to 10 share emails an hour.

Under **Links** you'll find every link made for the list, shown by its first few characters
(for example "Link q7Kd2X…"), with the date it was created and whether it has expired. To stop a
link working, press **Revoke**. Revoking takes effect straight away, and it also covers links
sent by email.

## 5. Other branches and quick theme lookup

**Other branches** (in **More**) shows, for each branch, its upcoming setlists, the songs it added
recently, and its most-used songs over the last 90 days. Use **Jump to branch** to go straight to
one.

**Quick theme lookup** (in **More**) is for the moment someone shares a word and you need songs
for it fast. Type a theme or words from a lyric into the large box. You get the top 12 matches in
big, easy-to-read type that you can hold up for someone else to see. **Show full** lists all the
matches, and **See all in the library** opens them as a normal search.

## 6. Adding and editing songs

_Contributors, music leaders and admins._ The [tagging guide](./tagging-guide.md) explains how
to choose energy levels, themes and the rest consistently.

Choose **Add song** (in **More**, or at the top of the library). To edit a song, use **Edit
song** on its page or row panel. The form has six tabs:

- **Basic info**: title (required), alternate titles, composers (at least one, in order),
  source group and year composed.
- **Keys & tempo**: the usual key first, then any other keys, each with its first chords; tempo
  (BPM), time signature, energy level and energy notes.
- **Lyrics**: chorus, verses 1–5 and bridge, plus the arrangement (the order they're sung,
  repeats allowed). **Paste full lyrics** splits a pasted song into sections if it has headings
  like "Verse 1" and "Chorus" on their own lines.
- **Chords (optional)**: tick **Add chords over the lyrics** to write chords into the lyrics. Put
  each chord in square brackets just before the syllable it's played on, for example
  `[G]Amazing [C]grace` (mid-word works too: `won[Em]derful`). A preview under each section shows
  the chords above the words.
  - **Each key has its own chords.** Pick a key under **Chords for** (the song's keys from Keys &
    tempo). The words are shared: change them in one key and they change in every key, with that
    key's chords moved to follow. Check them after big edits.
  - A key with no chords yet can **Start from the lyrics**, or **Fill from** another key, which
    transposes that key's chords as a starting point: every chord moves by the same number of
    half steps and keeps its type (Em in G becomes F♯m in A). You can only fill a major key from a
    major key, and a minor key from a minor key.
  - Before filling, choose how notes are named. **Spell by the key** (the default) names each
    note the way the new key's scale does, so in C a chord a whole step below C is **B♭**, not
    A♯. **All sharps** or **All flats** write every raised or lowered note one way, if your
    band's charts prefer that.
  - **Paste full lyrics** puts the paste (and any chords in it) into the key you're editing. A
    chord sheet with the chords on their own lines above the words works too.
  - **Remove chords in G** removes one key's chords; unticking the box removes them all. The
    lyrics stay either way.
  - On the song page, choosing a key under **Keys and first chords** shows that key's chords over
    the lyrics (or says there are none for it), and the key buttons next to **Chords** do the
    same. The **Chords** button shows or hides them. In the player, musicians get the
    setlist's key first.
  - The song page also has **Autoscroll** above the lyrics, with the same 1–10 **Speed** as Start
    event. The speed is remembered for each song on your device, in both places.
- **Themes**: tag each lyric section with themes from the shared list. You can create a new theme
  if none fits.
- **Branch usage**: how often each branch sings the song (high, low, rare or none). Usage is
  worked out from setlist history over the last year, and a level you choose overrides it.
- **Files & links**: upload chord charts, lead sheets, full scores and recordings for each key,
  or add YouTube, audio, video and instruction links. Projection files (`.pro`, `.mdb`, `.ppt`,
  `.pptx`) or a projection link go in the **Projection** block, which applies to every key. Save
  a new song first, then add files.

The branch that added a song is recorded automatically from your branch. Every change shows on
the song's **History** tab.

Admins can load many songs at once from a spreadsheet; see [Importing songs in bulk](./import.md).

## 7. Reporting a bug

Anyone can report a problem. In the account menu choose **Report a bug**, then fill in:

- **What went wrong?**: a short title, such as "the song list doesn't load".
- **What happened?**: what you did, step by step, and what you saw.
- What you expected to happen (optional).
- Up to 5 screenshots. You can paste them straight in.

The page you were on, your browser, your screen size and the app version are added for you.
After you press **Send report**, you can follow its status (**New**, **Confirmed**, **Fixed**,
**Won't fix** or **Duplicate**) and read any note from an admin on **Your bug reports**, which
is linked from the top of the report form.

## 8. Admin tools

Open **Admin** from the account menu. Music leaders see the **Themes** card only. Admins see:

- **Users**: approve or reject access requests (choose the person's role and branch first; for
  organization sign-ins the branch is suggested), invite people by email, change roles and
  branches, and disable or enable accounts. The card shows how many requests are waiting, and
  admins get an email for each new one. You can't disable your own account, and there must
  always be at least one active admin.
- **Branches**: add and rename branches, and set the sign-in group each one maps to.
- **Themes** (music leaders too): find a theme, **Merge** a duplicate into the theme to keep, edit
  its **Synonyms** (extra words that find it in search), or **Load starter vocabulary**.
- **Component gallery**: every screen and component of the app shown with made-up sample data, so
  you can check how things look and behave in each state (loading, empty, errors). It never
  shows real data, and only admins can open it.

Below the cards, admins also see:

- **To-dos**: a shared checklist for the admin team. Type in **What needs doing?** to add one,
  tick it off when it's done, or delete it. Outstanding items from the project's owner to-do list
  appear here automatically.
- **Enhancement requests**: ideas and changes for the app. Choose **New request**, pick the kind
  (**Enhancement** or **Change**), give a title and description, and attach up to 5 wireframe
  images (PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, up to 10 MB each). Each request has a status: **New**,
  **Planned**, **In progress**, **Done** or **Declined**. **View all requests** lists them all,
  and you can edit or delete any of them.
- **Bug reports**: the newest open reports and how many are open. Open a report to see its
  details and screenshots, set its status, and leave a note the reporter can read. Admins get an
  email for every new report.
